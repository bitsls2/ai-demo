## Where Chollet and ARC fit

Use a short bridge near the beginning of the technology section, not a separate long lecture about leaderboards. ARC's original 2019 paper concerns generalisation and skill-acquisition efficiency. That gives a reason to ask precise questions about what a test establishes, rather than calling all apparent intelligence or all failure the same thing. See [On the Measure of Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01547).

The supplied Chollet post contributes a different proposition: imperfect reasoning models may enable productive new workflows for inspection, tests, visualisation and challenge. It is an account of his practice, not a controlled DWP evaluation. The exact user-supplied excerpt is:

> not because LRMs got perfect but because they're so fast at doing things that you can develop new workflows around them

Source supplied by the owner: https://x.com/fchollet/status/2104598248501699067 . The public web fetch returned HTTP 403 during preparation. Quote the supplied text with attribution, but do not claim independent verification of the complete live post or infer its publication timestamp. A genuine capture of the post can be added only if available through an authorised browser; do not fabricate an X card.

Proposed transition, not a quotation:

> We are not asking you to stop reading the guidance. We are asking whether AI can help you read it, follow its dependencies and challenge the system more effectively—while preserving access to the original evidence.

That preserves the business focus. Coding is the example in Chollet's post, not the subject of this presentation. Likewise ARC is an analogy for disciplined evaluation, not a certification of OKF-DWP or an entitlement benchmark.

Do not automatically copy the earlier chat's full chronology, percentages, model names or cost estimates into the deck. Any numeric ARC material needs its own primary-source check with benchmark version, evaluation split, named model/system, harness, run date, publication/update date, and whether cost is per task or per whole evaluation. Do not make one continuous percentage chart across ARC-AGI-1/2 and ARC-AGI-3. ARC-AGI-3's official launch describes an interactive benchmark; that supports a qualitative distinction, not cross-benchmark score equivalence. [ARC-AGI-3 launch](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-agi-3-launch).

## Claims to avoid

Do not say “all the law is in the bundle”, “all 40 questions are answerable”, “the system cannot hallucinate”, “WebMCP works in every browser”, “the calculation is now automated”, or “the screenshots prove better decisions”. Nor should technical integrity percentages be plotted as answer-accuracy percentages. A source-linked output can still be incomplete or wrongly interpreted.

Suggested closing, presented as the speaker's synthesis rather than a claim measured by the repo:

> The opportunity is not to remove judgement. It is to make the evidence, transformations and unanswered questions easier to inspect—so that judgement has something better to work with.
