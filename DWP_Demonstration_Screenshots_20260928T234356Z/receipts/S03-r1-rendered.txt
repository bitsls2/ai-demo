Introduction

60025 [See DMG memo 12/23] [See DMG memo 12-25] The conditions for entitlement are that the
claimant


1. is engaged in caring for a severely disabled person for any day1 (see DMG 60035 - 60042) and


2. is regularly and substantially engaged in caring for that person 2 (see DMG 60035 - 60042) and


3. is not gainfully employed3 (see DMG 60051 - 60052) and


4. is aged 16 or over4 (see DMG 60058) and


5. is not in FTE5 (see DMG 60068 - 60081) and


6. satisfies prescribed conditions of residence or presence in GB6 (see DMG Chapter 07 Part 2).
