# Demo 1 - Overview

Using the OKF bundle for the decision makers guide answer the following questions.

[decision-makers-guide-staff-guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/decision-makers-guide-staff-guide}

## High level Plan 

- Create an OKF bundle for the staff guide. 

- Summarize any problems in creating the OKF Bundle. For example conflicting guidance and omitted guidance.     

- Use the OKF Bundle to answer several questions below. For several of the questions repeat the process both with and without the OKF bundle. Demonstrate that the OKF bundle uses less tokens and improved results.  

## Sample Questions

### General

- What happens to your benefits if you go abroad?
 
### State Pension

- If your birthday is 30th May 1960, what is the first date you will be entitled to State Pension?
- How does State Pension interact with PIP?
- What are the components which make up State Pension and how do you qualify for them?
- What other benefits could you receive in addition to State Pension, and under what circumstances would you be entitled to them?
 
###  Pension Credit

- What is the maximum you can have in savings to still qualify for Pension Credit?
- What were the Pension Credit weekly rates over the last five years?
- What was the SDA amount over the last five years?
- Besides SDA, what are the other additional payments which you can receive in addition to basic Pension Credit, and what have been their rates over the last five years?
- What is used in the Pension Credit calculation to assess eligibility and what is the threshold for each element?
- How does Housing Benefit impact Pension Credit?
- Does Pension Credit stop is a citizen moves into a care home permanently if they are self-funding?
- Does Pension Credit stop is a citizen moves into a care home permanently if they are not self-funding?
- What is the impact of someone’s partner on their Pension Credit?
- What inputs are required for the Pension Credit calculation?
- Given these inputs what is the formula for calculating Pension Credit?
- What difference to Pension Credit does having a partner make?
- What happens if someone with a partner goes permanently into a Care Home (please give all scenarios)?
- How does Pension Credit work?
- How does a citizen qualify for Pension Credit?
- What are the eligibility rules and entitlement rate for PC?
- How do State Pension and Pension Credit fit together. For example: If someone receives State Pension, could they also be entitled to Pension Credit?
- What happens to Pension Credit if someone moves abroad?
  
### Industrial Injuries Benefits

- What additional benefits would you be entitled to if you are entitled to IIDB?
- What happens if you are on IIDB when you reach State Pension Age?
- What is the interaction between Child DLA and PIP?
- Can you receive both IIDB and JSA?
- Can you receive both IIDB and ESA?
- What is the relationship between IIDB, PIP, Attendance Allowance and DLA?
- How does IIDB relate to PIP?
 
### Personal Independence Payment

- What additional benefits would you be entitled to if you are entitled to PIP?
- What happens if you are on PIP when you reach State Pension Age?
- What is the interaction between Child DLA and PIP?
- Can you receive both PIP and JSA?
- Can you receive both PIP and ESA?
- What is the relationship between PIP, Attendance Allowance and DLA?
- If you receive PIP and War Pensions Constant Allowance, how much would you get each week (please provide all variations)?
 
### Carers Allowance

- Under what circumstances can a Citizen claim Carers Allowance?
- What impact does Carers Allowance have on other benefits which are being claimed?
- What happens to Carers Allowance if someone is caring for two or more people?
